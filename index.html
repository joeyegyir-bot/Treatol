Questions

Before we visit.

How often should the house be treated or cleaned?

Weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. The most requested rhythm is a fortnightly visit — we will schedule to the life of the household, not the other way around.

Do you bring the supplies?

Yes. When we arrive we are equipped with the cleaning supplies, equipment and formulations needed to treat the property thoroughly.

Is there a contract?

Services are offered without a locked-in contract and can be modified at any time. Tell us what changed; we will adjust the brief.

Are the products safe around food?

Our green-based, non-toxic products are certified for use in and around food processing areas, with no rinsing required.

Do you work outside Accra?

Yes. Fumigation and pest-control technicians deliver solutions throughout Ghana — residential and commercial.
