Questions

Before we visit.

How do I use Pesticine?

Spray around all the sides and edges of the treatment area, and any other infested area. Keep out of reach of children. Do not inhale the mist. Wash hands after handling.

How often should the house be treated or cleaned?

Weekly, every two weeks, or monthly. The most requested rhythm is a fortnightly visit — we schedule to the life of the household.

Do you bring the supplies?

Yes. When we arrive we are equipped with the cleaning supplies, equipment and formulations needed to treat the property thoroughly.

Is there a contract?

Services are offered without a locked-in contract and can be modified at any time.

Do you work outside Accra?

Yes. Fumigation and pest-control technicians deliver solutions throughout Ghana — residential and commercial.
